## Observations

- **Scripted settle time.** The first `--shot` after a far or long walk waits for the world in view: 32 s at `--prefetch 2` and 54 s at
  `--prefetch 0` (runs 1, 8, 16, 17; `scene draw longest` 31.7 to 54.5 s). The live path has no such frame: the start is the worst at
  520 to 720 ms (runs 2, 22). Run 16 and run 17 (prefetch 0 and 2, the same script) produce byte-identical saves (world digest
  `1d6ce8d9bc77c10a`), so residency and prefetch do not change results, as the brief requires.
- **Earlier "teleport hitches" are retracted.** A 12.6 s frame (run 8, teleport to 700,700), 1.9 s (run 3) and 1.96 s (run 4) are the
  settled shots, not the teleport: the same far teleport on the live path (run 22) has no frame over 720 ms after the start.
- **The closed pass stops the body at the rockfall or the valley wall.** Run 4: `walk-to 383,208` with the pass closed gave up at
  (355.7, 205.3) after 14,400 ticks: the body is at the rockfall's west face (`scene.json` stamp `rockfall` at x 355, z 200, 17 by 17).
  Run 24: `walk-to 372,196` (north of the rockfall) gave up at (343.7, 196.7) after 14,400 ticks, so the valley's edge wall held the body
  north of the rockfall too (`/tmp/vocaterra-qa/w24/north.png`: the view is a black vertical band, which I could not identify from one
  picture; it may be the wall seen edge-on). A give-up costs 14,400 ticks, about 3 to 4 minutes of real time per run.
- **With the pass open the walk runs through.** `--pass open` plus `walk-to 330,217`, `383,208`, `420,208` arrives at each point (run 5,
  live path, 1,794 frames; its frame times are contaminated by B12's run).
- **Drowning.** Run 12 walked into the lake at (212, 268) and waited 20 s there: the body drowned at tick 1618 (`player_drowned`) and
  woke at the camp (174,43,188). That is the breath rule working (the scripted r31 walk leaves the water at once); not a bug.
- **Current.** In the pool at the waterfall foot the body moved from (258, 38) to (262, 133) in 25 s of waiting (run 21). I did not verify
  that it was carried by a current rather than walking; it did not drown there.
- **Placing and reloading far out.** Planks placed at (703, 98, 700), 700,700 with `--pass open` survive a save and load (run 9 to run 10:
  `load: world digest 9275da570e792208 = the save's (restored equal)`, the planks visible after the load, `/tmp/vocaterra-qa/w10/east.png`).
  Breaking them is refused by the land rule (run 11: "planks come back only on the player's own land"), which is Y13's design, not a bug.
  The world digest changes on a re-save with no edits, because the state digest includes the tick and the body pose
  (`VoxelSimulation.ComputeStateDigest`); this is expected.
- **Cave entrance.** The preview tool (`entrances --world valley --x 230 --z 200 --size 512`) draws open columns at about (173 to 180,
  447 to 455) and (455 to 465, 29 to 46). Teleport to 176,449 stands at y 89; `walk-to 175,452` takes the body into the shaft to y 80, inside
  a cave with stone walls and ore spots (`/tmp/vocaterra-qa/w20/inside.png`); no fall-through.
- **Far view and haze look right** in the samples: far land at 700,700 with snow on the far ridge (`/tmp/vocaterra-qa/w8/far700.png`); a
  dusk, rain, low-preset view with haze over the far mountains, the far view to 4 km (`/tmp/vocaterra-qa/w18/dusk-rain-low.png`); the lake
  bank and the water (`/tmp/vocaterra-qa/w14/a.png`); the waterfall from the pool (`/tmp/vocaterra-qa/w7/foot-up.png`).
- **`--world valley`** at night (`--time 0.5 --weather cloudy`) runs, walks to 330,217 (44 turns) and back, no streamed line, no errors
  (run 19, `/tmp/vocaterra-qa/w19/night-valley.png`).
- **Stream stats** on the live path: no frame over 100 ms after the start in the no-settle runs (runs 2, 22); the walks through the
  streamed land had 112 to 225 ms frames (runs 5, 12, 20), which I could not separate from B12's load.
- The flag help says yaw 0 is north and 90 east, pitch positive up (`--look`); the scripted-run docs should match.
