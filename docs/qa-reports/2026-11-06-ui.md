## Bugs

### QA-2026-11-06-ui-1: the expanded panel silently cuts off the oldest lines (major, game)

- **Command (reproduces at the default size):**
  `--seed 42 --mute --talk shopkeeper --say "quiero agua, por favor" --say "sí, por favor" --say "gracias" --expand --screenshot <png> --frames 10`
  (also `--talk fisher --say "¿dónde está la panadería?" --say "¿dónde está el río?" --say "gracias" --expand`, run `fish`; and the 8-turn run `t8`)
- **Expected:** after `--expand` (or PageUp / O) every older line of the conversation is reachable. The doc (`docs/game/running.md`, U02) says the key "shows them", and the dialogue model's comment says the expanded page is `MaxLines` (60) lines.
- **Actual:** the panel draws only the newest rows that fit (`DrawLines` takes `first = rows.Count - capacity`). When the history is longer than the panel, the top lines vanish and the "N earlier lines hidden" row is not drawn either (it is only inserted when `rows.Count < capacity`). In `s3/expanded.png` the first exchange (`quiero agua, por favor`, `Toma la botella…`, `¿Quieres pan?`) is gone and only 8 rows show; in `t8/expanded.png` only the last 6 lines show with no hint that more exist. The learner cannot read the start of a longer conversation anywhere in the game except the book's "What I said".
- **Evidence:** `/tmp/vocaterra-qa/ui2/s3/expanded.png`, `/tmp/vocaterra-qa/ui2/t8/expanded.png`, `/tmp/vocaterra-qa/ui2/fish/shot.png` (the `You: ¿dónde está la panadería?` line is missing). Code: `src/Vocaterra.Game/UI/Dialogue/DialoguePanel.cs` (`DrawLines`, the `first` and the `dialogueOlder` insert); `src/Vocaterra.Game.Core/Dialogue/DialogueModel.cs` (`PanelRows`, expanded branch).
- **Owner:** game (`Vocaterra.Game` drawing; the model's expanded rows are right).

## Observations

These are not bugs in the sense of the report, but a learner would trouble over them.

1. **A wrong or off-prompt line gets a stock reply that reads like a dead end.** `quiero pan` after the bread was sold gets `¡Nos vemos!` (goodbye) with the feedback `understood` (run `t8`, turn 7). `adiós`, `hola`, `gracias`, `no, gracias` all get `Te entiendo. ¿Quieres comprar algo?`. Both are the shopkeeper's fallback, and the coaching line does not tell the learner what to say (`/tmp/vocaterra-qa/ui2/t8/stdout.txt`).
2. **A slip inside a lesson step shows its recast in the strip only.** `ola` at step 3 (`Say it`) gives `English: Not quite. Say: Hola. It means hello.` in the strip, and the panel shows only `You: ola` (`/tmp/vocaterra-qa/ui2/les2/slip.png`). The panel's one coaching line (U02) is not there for a lesson slip. Whether that is intended is for the owner.
3. **A greeting other than the step's target is accepted.** `buenos días` at the lesson's `Say hello back: "Hola"` step is `understood` and moves on to step 4 (`/tmp/vocaterra-qa/ui2/les2/offprompt.png`). That is a fair greeting, but the step asked for `Hola`.
4. **The baker's slip recast is clear.** `yo quiere dos pan` is understood at 60 % with `Claro, «yo quiero dos panes». Muy bien.` in the panel, and the sale goes through (`/tmp/vocaterra-qa/ui2/bake/slip.png`). Good; noted as a check that passed.
5. **Settings panel labels.** The row reads `Screen effects`, while the doc (`docs/game/running.md`, N09) and the flags say "view effects". A learner reading the panel does not see the word the docs use. Cosmetic.
6. **Settings panel at its extremes fits.** `×0.5` and `×4` sit at the ends of the slider (`/tmp/vocaterra-qa/ui2/set3/shot.png`, `set2/shot.png`); at 800x600 with 150 % text the rows, the hint line and the border all fit. The toggles read `on`/`off` and the relaxed-needs toggle reads `on` after `--relaxed-needs`. Nothing else seen.
7. **`--needs-rate` refusal is clean.** `0.7` is refused with exit 2 and the message names the range and the step (run `set0`).
