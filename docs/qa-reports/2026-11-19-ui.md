## Bugs

### QA-2026-11-19-ui-1 (major, game)
- Reproduce (crafting panel is taller than the window at 1280x720; no scroll): `--size 1280x720 --text-scale 100 --crafting --wear axe=300 --wear jacket=200 --give cloth --give leather --give iron --give wood --give flour --equip lantern --equip shield`
  and (same at the default 1080p size): `--size 1920x1080 --text-scale 150 --crafting --wear axe=300 --give wood --give cloth --equip lantern`
- Expected: every recipe row and the repair rows are reachable; the footer ("C or Esc: close · click a row: make") and the panel's bottom border are on screen; or the list scrolls.
- Actual: the header says "You can make: 0 of 24" (1280x720), and only 23 of the 24 rows are on screen: the last row is off the bottom, with the footer. At 1920x1080 150 % all 23 rows are drawn (the last is `hacha (repair)`), but the panel's bottom border and the footer are off the screen, and any row added after it would be cut as well. The same panel fits at 1920x1080 100 % with no repair row (22 rows, footer shown, `craft3`). So the panel fits until a damaged tool or gear adds a repair row (the repair rows come last), and then the footer and the last row or rows are cut.
- Evidence: `.../qa-ui/craft1/shot.png` (the last visible row `hacha (repair)`, the 24th row and the footer below the screen), `.../qa-ui/craft2/shot.png` (no footer, no bottom border), `.../qa-ui/craft3/shot.png` (fits).
- Likely file: `src/Vocaterra.Game/UI/Crafting/CraftingLayout.cs` (`Height` from `RowCount`, the row height clamped to a minimum of 24, `Y` clamped to 8); `CraftingLayer.cs` (no scroll, footer at `panel.Bottom - 26`).

### QA-2026-11-19-ui-3 (minor, game)
- Reproduce: `--size 1280x720 --text-scale 100 --inventory --equip axe`

- Actual: "equip: axe refused (NotWearable)" and the axe is in neither the bag nor the worn list (`axe1/save.json`: the bag holds only the book). The code's comment in `VoxelGame.cs` (`--equip` handler) says "a refusal gives nothing", so the help text and the code disagree. - Evidence: `.../qa-ui/axe1/out.txt`, `.../qa-ui/axe1/save.json`.

## Observations
- `--equip` silently displaces the item that holds a slot (the starting shirt and the shield both went to the bag, with only "worn in ..." printed). A test aid, so fine, but the console could say what went to the bag.
- At 800x600 and 640x480 the panel covers the whole hotbar (it is an overlay, so this is expected; the off-hand cell stays visible above the hotbar).
- The third-person figure draws the cosmetic jacket over the cuirass (cosmetic first, as plan 3.11 says), so the armour's body piece is hidden by a cosmetic clothing piece; by design, but a learner who wears armour under a cosmetic jacket sees no armour.
- In third person I could not confirm that the off-hand shield shows in the left hand; the figure is small in the frame (`third1/shot.png`, `third1/avatar-zoom.png`).
- The held hat, when picked up, is drawn across the right edge of its origin cell (`mv2/shot.png`). Small; the origin cell is left empty.
- The crafting repair label is in English (`(repair)`) inside the Spanish row, as the other UI words are English in the English locale. Consistent, but a learner sees an English word in the Spanish list.
- The panel's footer reads "shift: wear" at 1280x720 and "shift+click: wear or move" at 1920x1080; the short hints drop "move" at the small sizes (the long line does not fit). Small.
