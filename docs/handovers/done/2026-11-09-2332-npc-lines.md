## The bug and its cause
- **Symptom (issue #53):** you ask the shopkeeper for water ("Quiero agua, por favor"). The panel shows «Toma la botella. El agua
  está en el río.» (or «Aquí tienes la botella. …», the other variant of the `shop_give` pool) and «¿Quieres pan?», but he does
  not say them.
- **Not missing audio:** `content/game-v1/speech/shopkeeper/shop_give.1.wav`, `shop_give.2.wav` and `shop_offer.1.wav` (and the
  `.slow` files) are present, valid (16-bit mono 24 kHz) and listed in `content/speech-index.json`.
- **Cause:** the turn and the bottle's first word card arrive in the same frame. `TurnPresenter.Speak` queues the turn's lines with
  `NpcVoice.Say`. Straight after, `WordCardPresenter.AfterTick` (the tick observer for first pickups) also called
  `voice.Say(SpeechSpeakers.Guide, [card.Phrase])`. `Say` stops the clip that plays and drops the queue. So `shop_give.N` was cut off
  as soon as it started, `shop_offer.1` was dropped, and only `card.botella` was heard. A scripted run's log showed it:
  ```
  turn 1 (shopkeeper, stall-need): "quiero agua, por favor" -> request_item ... host events stall.give_water
    shopkeeper: Toma la botella. El agua está en el río.
    shopkeeper: ¿Quieres pan?
  voice: shop_give.1
  voice: card.botella        <- replaced the queue
  ```

## Completed (commit `cd104a9c`)
- `src/Vocaterra.Game.Core/Session/WordCardPresenter.cs`: a word card's noun is now queued with
  `voice.Append(SpeechSpeakers.Guide, card.Phrase, slow: false)`, after what plays, instead of `Say`. The phrase-support cues
  (`SupportFrontend.Pump`) already used `Append`.
- `src/Vocaterra.Game.Core/Sound/NpcVoice.cs`: two new console log lines:
  - `voice: cut off <id>[, N queued dropped]` (or `cut off after <id>, N queued dropped` when the clip had ended but the queue was
    dropped), written by `Stop()` when a clip is stopped before its end or queued lines are dropped. It tracks the id that plays
    in `_current`.
  - `voice: text only (<npc>) <text>`: a shown line that has no recording (the `TextOnly` counter already counted these).
- Tests:
  - New: `NpcVoiceTests.TheCardOfAnItemAnNpcHandsOverWaitsForHisWholeTurn` runs the real exchange through `VoxelSession` and
    `WordCardPresenter`. Expected: `shop_give.*`, `shop_offer.1`, `card.botella`, with no stop. It was checked to fail with the
    old `Say` (2 clips instead of 3).
  - `NpcVoiceTests`: `Played()` filter skips the new log lines; asserts added for the cut-off log (`ANewUtteranceReplacesTheClipThatPlays`), its absence in the key-words test, and the text-only log line.
  - `BookSpeechTests` (`Played()` helper): its filter skips `voice: cut off` and `voice: text only` (3 tests failed without this).

## Review of the other NPCs (the issue's second request)
Scripted runs (`--screenshot … --quality low` plus steps) of: all 12 lessons (`--lesson-run <id>`), the shopkeeper with "sí, por
favor" and with "no, gracias", and `--talk` with the villager, fisher, baker, smith, farmer and guide ("hola", "¿qué tiempo
hace?", "adiós").
- No `voice: missing`, no `voice: text only` and no `voice: fallback` in any run: every NPC line shown has its recording.
- Many `voice: cut off` lines, all caused by the script and not by the game. Scripted runs tick once per frame at about 2 ms a
  frame, so game time runs about 8× faster than real time, and the script answers, presses Continue or walks off (`--lesson-run`
  does not print its walks) while the NPC is still talking. The rest come from the game closing at the end of a run.
- One check looked for the signature of this bug (a clip cut off with no player action between its start and the cut-off). It
  found only those walk-away and end-of-run cases.
- Behaviour that is by design: closing the dialogue (walking away) stops the voice (`DialogueScreen` calls `_voice.Stop()`), and a new turn
  replaces what is still playing.
