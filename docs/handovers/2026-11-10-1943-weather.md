## Outcome

The owner asked for a modular weather system (sunny, cloudy and rain first) and chose a free-running day and night cycle as its first
phase. The plan was approved on 2026-11-10. Nine of its ten milestones are gated and pushed; the last, E08 (the close-out), is running,
and the pull request to `main` follows it.

What the game does on `feature/weather` today:

- A free-running day and night: a 40-minute cycle (about 30 minutes of light, 10 of night), a new game at 8:00, a sleep or a collapse
  waking at 7:00. The sky, the sun, the moon, the stars, the fog and the world's light follow it; lanterns glow by their own layer.
- Weather as rows of data (`content/game-v1/game/weather.json`: `sunny`, `cloudy`, `rain`) that changes by itself in seeded spells with
  a 45-second blend, and is rolled anew on waking. The sky, the clouds, the fog and the light follow it; rain is drawn (not under
  roofs or trees) and heard; the wind rises and the birds go quiet.
- Asked «¿Qué tiempo hace?», every NPC answers with the weather the world has.
- Testing: `--time <hour>`, `--weather <id>`, `--dusk`, the menu's Time of day and Weather rows, the F3 line, the scripted steps
  `--set-time` and `--set-weather`.
- No gameplay effect of weather, by the owner's decision for the first version.
