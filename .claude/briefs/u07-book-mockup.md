## Design direction
Hierarchy by size (16 and 32 only), ink weight, spacing and less text. One row per entry in the list; the counts and long text move to the right page, which already shows the selected item. Known items read strongest, unmet ones fade, meta text is clearly lighter and not the unmet colour. Each page's ribbon colour as its accent (title rule, selection). Sections where the page has groups (Nouns: Known, Learning, Not met yet; What I said: per session). Lessons collapsed to one row each, the current lesson marked. Keep the book's character (leather cover, cream paper, ribbons): this is a hierarchy pass, not a new look.
